📖 How to use our Wishing Video Cards

Creating a beautiful WhatsApp video status on wishmaker.in is extremely easy and 100% free. Follow these simple steps to generate your video card:

  • Step 1: Select a category from above (Birthday, Anniversary, Festivals) and browse our templates.
  • Step 2: Click the "Play" icon to watch the sample video. Once you like a design, click on the "🚀 Use This Card" button.
  • Step 3: You will be redirected to our editor. Enter the Name and upload a nice Photo.
  • Step 4: Submit the form and your magical video is ready! Download the video or share it directly to your WhatsApp Status.
